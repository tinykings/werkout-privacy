Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026

Werkowt has no advertising, does not track you, and does not sell your personal information.

Information you enter

Werkowt lets you enter workout plans, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout history, body-weight entries, gym codes, and related fitness information. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Storage and iCloud sync

Your information is stored on your device. When iCloud is available, Werkowt uses Apple CloudKit to sync information through your private iCloud database so it can remain available across your devices. The developer does not operate a separate account system or server for this information and cannot access records in your private iCloud database. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Information sharing

Werkowt does not sell or rent your information. Information leaves the app only when needed for iCloud sync or when you choose to share or export it. For example, you may create a workout QR code or export a JSON backup. Anyone who receives such a QR code or backup may be able to read the workout information it contains.

Third-party websites

Werkowt can open an exercise search on YouTube in a Safari view. Search terms and activity on that website are handled by YouTube, Google, and Apple according to their respective privacy policies. Werkowt does not receive your YouTube browsing history or account information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Werkowt does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. It does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device and, when sync is enabled, in your private iCloud database until you delete it. You can delete individual plans, workouts, exercises, and body-weight entries in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data, but synced information may remain in iCloud or on your other devices. You can manage iCloud data through your device’s Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Children’s privacy

Werkowt does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer-operated service. Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use of fitness apps and any external websites opened from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Werkowt’s features or legal obligations change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to thetinykings@icloud.com.

Support

Need help with Werkowt, iCloud sync, importing a backup, or reporting a problem? Email support and include your iOS version, Werkowt version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include sensitive health or fitness information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Common troubleshooting

  • Confirm your device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is enabled.
  • Check your internet connection if changes are not syncing.
  • Close and reopen Werkowt after changing iCloud settings.
  • Use the import/export control in the Log tab to create or restore a JSON backup.
